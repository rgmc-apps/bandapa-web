**Key Features:**

1. **Background**: Dark studio (#0A0A0A) with 10 warm amber/orange bokeh circles at varying sizes and opacities, simulating stage lighting

2. **Phone Mockup**: 
   - 360x720px iPhone-style frame, centered on canvas
   - Matte black with subtle inner shadow
   - Notch at top for modern look
   - Clipped content area for proper display

3. **App Content**:
   - Status bar with time and signal indicators
   - "Profile" header with settings gear icon
   - Premium profile card with amber ring avatar showing "E" initial
   - User info: Erwin Arellano, @erwin404, Manila, Philippines
   - Filled amber pills for skills (Guitarist, Vocalist)
   - Outlined pills for genres (Rock, Indie, Alternative)
   - Basic Info section with input fields
   - Artist Info section with instrument and genre pill grid
   - Save Profile button (amber, full-width pill shape)

4. **Design Details**:
   - Professional typography hierarchy
   - Proper color scheme (#FFA500 amber accents, #141414 dark background)
   - Rounded corners for modern feel
   - Subtle borders and dividers
   - All text is readable and properly positioned

The design is production-ready and follows modern mobile app design standards!
